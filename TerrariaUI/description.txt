Arima-UI Terraria

Liquid Glass interface for Terraria.

Replaces the vanilla interface with a dark, glassy design system: rounded translucent
panels with a 1 px light rim, moulded pill buttons, recessed slots, a dimmed backdrop
behind open interfaces, a glass HUD, a recoloured cursor set, and an optional style-guide
screen for inspecting every component.

Pairs with the "Arima-UI Terraria" resource pack, which skins the menus, settings and mod
config screens (tModLoader cannot repaint those from code).

Cosmetic only: no gameplay changes, no saves touched. Client side.
